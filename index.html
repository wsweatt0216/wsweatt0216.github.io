Everything lives in your right-click menu

Snip to PDF

Drag a box around anything on screen. It lands in the current folder as a PDF — the step this app exists to delete.

Snip to PNG / Clipboard

Same snip, saved as an image file — or sent to your clipboard to paste anywhere, started with your mouse instead of the keyboard.

Paste as PDF or PNG

Already have an image copied? Save it straight into the folder as a real file.

Combine Pages

Right-click the folder, click files in the order you want the pages, name it. Every page comes out the same size, and snips become searchable text — automatically.

Compress

Select the heavy PDFs, right-click, and smaller copies appear beside them. Text pages stay razor sharp, everything stays searchable, and your originals are never modified.

Honest by design

If a PDF can't get meaningfully smaller, Compress says so instead of handing you a junk copy. Duplicates get (2) — nothing is ever overwritten.

Quick name box

Enter keeps the suggested date name. Esc discards. Every save is named before it lands, so folders stay tidy.

Private by design

Runs entirely on your PC. No network access, no accounts, no telemetry. Privacy policy.
