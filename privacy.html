Snip + Paste — Privacy Policy

Savvy-Apps · Effective July 16, 2026

The short version

Snip + Paste collects nothing. It runs entirely on your device, has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and makes no network connections.

What the app touches, and why

Your clipboard — read only when you choose a Paste command, and written only when you choose Snip to Clipboard. The app never reads the clipboard in the background.

Your screen — captured only when you choose a Snip command, using the standard Windows snipping overlay, and only the region you select.

Your files — PDFs and images are created only in the folder you right-clicked, with the name you approve. The Combine command reads only the files you selected.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app has no server, transmits no data, and stores nothing outside the files it creates for you.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this page and the app's Store listing will say so plainly before you update.
